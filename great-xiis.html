Great XIs

Three shapes that have gone deep. Steal the shape, not the names — your reels will never land exactly these cells.

The Almanack XI

Hutton (50s) and Gavaskar (70s) to open; Bradman (30s), Richards (80s) and Sobers (60s) in the engine room; Gilchrist (00s) at seven; then Warne (90s), Marshall (80s), Lillee (70s) and McGrath (00s). Balance everywhere, no passengers, all five conditions covered.

The Fortress (spin-first)

Built for turners: two 60s openers who play spin, a middle order from the subcontinent’s golden 2000s, Murali and Warne in tandem, one enforcer quick. On a Subcontinent Tour this shape has the best sweep odds in the pool.

The Battery (pace-first)

Four quicks — Marshall, Holding, Lillee, Steyn — a keeper who stands back well, and batters picked purely for how they played pace. Brutal Down Under; fragile in Galle. Commit or don’t.
