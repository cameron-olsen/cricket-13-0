The strategy guide

How the 13-0s are actually built.

Build for the tour, not the trophy cabinet

The tour type is named before you draft. A Subcontinent Tour wants two spinners and batters who play the turning ball; Down Under wants height and pace. The same XI that sweeps one map gets rolled on another.

Take twenty wickets

Draws kill perfect seasons more often than defeats. Batting depth feels safe, but four genuine bowlers — and a spinner you trust on day five — is what turns 1-0 leads into wins.

The keeper is not optional

You can field an XI without gloves, and the engine will make you regret it: chances go down all tour. One keeper, placed early, never hurts.

Small samples lie

A 60 average over nine Tests is regression bait — the engine damps short careers toward the middle. A 50 average over eighty Tests is the real thing.

Spend the re-spins late

Early cells always offer someone useful. Hold the nation and era re-spins for the last three picks, when the slot you must fill and the cell you’ve landed don’t match.
