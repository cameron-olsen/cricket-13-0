How to play

Eleven spins, eleven legends, thirteen Tests. Here is the whole game.

The draft

Each of your eleven picks starts with a spin: the reels land on a nation × decade — Australia in the 90s, the West Indies in the 80s. The list shows every player of that nation and decade; sort by runs, wickets or dismissals, search, then choose one and drop him into a slot. You get one nation re-spin and one era re-spin per draft, so spend them well.

Strict slots

The team sheet sets the shape: two openers, two top order, three middle order (one can instead be a bowler), a spinner, two fast bowlers and a flexible bowling slot. Roles are strict — an opener only fills an opener’s spot. All-rounders carry their real bowling type. A keeper isn’t compulsory, but going without one is heavily punished in the field.

Era-true numbers

A player brings only the decade you drafted him in. Bradman of the 30s and Bradman of the 40s are different cards. Under the hood every number is era-adjusted, so a hundred against 1980s pace is worth more than a flat-track hundred.

The tour

Then thirteen Tests, ball by ball, across turners, green tops, swing, flat roads and true decks. Open Tours roam the world against graded local XIs; Legend Tours take on a great side — the Invincibles, the 80s West Indies — in their own conditions, and you can’t draft from the side you’re touring. Tap any Test for its full scorecard. Every share link replays the exact tour, all thirteen scorecards included.
